# Executive Proposal

## Proposal headline

A phased digital growth engagement to improve execution speed, conversion performance, and commercial accountability without displacing the internal developer.

## Executive recommendation

Digital Heroes should propose a low-risk, phased program that begins with a paid diagnostic and moves into implementation only after the COO, internal developer, and commercial stakeholders agree on the roadmap.

This approach gives the COO confidence, gives the developer a clear role, and gives Digital Heroes a path to expand from advisory diagnosis into delivery, optimization, and performance marketing support.

## Business case

The client's current challenge is not simply a website problem. It is an execution system problem: commercial priorities, development capacity, campaign needs, analytics, and conversion improvements are not moving together quickly enough.

Digital Heroes can create value by:

* Identifying the highest-value revenue and conversion constraints.
* Translating commercial priorities into a practical delivery roadmap.
* Supporting the internal developer with specialist capacity and process.
* Improving campaign landing pages, tracking, and website performance.
* Giving the COO clearer reporting and governance.

## Recommended engagement

| Phase | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic | Shared clarity on opportunity, risks, priorities, and investment case. |
| Foundation | Critical platform, conversion, and reporting blockers removed. |
| Growth | Campaign experiences and optimization roadmap activated. |
| Retainer | Continuous improvement cadence established. |

## Strategic advantage

Digital Heroes should not compete as a commodity web vendor. The winning position is an integrated growth partner that connects website execution, Shopify expertise, and performance marketing judgment.

That position is strongest when the proposal emphasizes:

* COO-level business outcomes.
* Respectful technical collaboration.
* Measured rollout instead of risky big-bang transformation.
* Clear governance and reporting.
* Commercial options that protect both sides.

## Executive close

The recommended ask is approval for Phase 1. The close should be framed as:

“Let us validate the opportunity, align the internal team, and give you a board-ready roadmap before either side commits to a larger implementation.”

That close is credible, lowers perceived risk, and creates a natural path into the full program.
