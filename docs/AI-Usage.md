# AI Usage

## Purpose

AI assistance was used to accelerate the preparation of Task B deliverables while preserving human judgement over commercial strategy, deal logic, and final recommendations.

## Where AI was used

* Structuring Task B into clear deliverables across deal strategy, proposal outline, commercial structure, negotiation planning, and executive proposal.
* Drafting concise consulting-style language for stakeholder management, risk analysis, competitive positioning, and objection handling.
* Creating Mermaid diagram syntax for the sales process, stakeholder map, negotiation flow, and project timeline.
* Checking consistency across the required sections so that the deliverables align with the assignment brief.

## Where human judgement was applied

* Selecting the central deal strategy: win the COO while converting the internal developer from a potential blocker into a collaborator.
* Choosing a phased commercial model rather than a single large fixed-scope proposal.
* Defining relative pricing logic without inventing unsupported exact fees.
* Balancing competitive positioning against realistic agency delivery risks.
* Ensuring the writing remains professional, concise, original, and suitable for a business development qualification assignment.

## Limitations

The deliverables use reasonable business assumptions because no live client discovery notes, budget data, analytics exports, or stakeholder interviews were provided. Any final client-facing proposal should be validated through discovery before being priced or contracted.
